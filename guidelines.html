Liqaa — Community Guidelines

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

Liqaa exists so people can meet, talk, and work together comfortably. These guidelines describe what we expect from everyone using the service. They apply to meetings, chat messages, shared files, screen shares, display names, and profile information.

1. Treat People With Respect

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or intimidation.
  • No hate speech or attacks based on religion, ethnicity, nationality, gender, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
  • No stalking, doxxing, or sharing someone's private information without their consent.

2. Keep Content Appropriate

  • No sexual content involving minors, under any circumstances. This is reported and permanently banned.
  • No graphic violence, gore, or content designed to shock or disturb.
  • No nudity or sexually explicit material in meetings, chat, screen shares, or profile images.

3. No Illegal or Harmful Activity

  • Do not use Liqaa to plan, promote, or carry out illegal activity.
  • No selling or promoting weapons, illegal drugs, or stolen data.
  • No malware, phishing links, or attempts to compromise other users' devices or accounts.

4. Be Honest About Who You Are

  • Do not impersonate another person, brand, or organisation.
  • Do not use a display name designed to mislead other participants.
  • Do not create accounts to evade a block or a suspension.

5. Respect Privacy and Consent

  • Recording is local to the device of the person recording, and everyone in the meeting is notified when it starts. Ask for consent anyway — some places require it by law.
  • Do not share recordings, screenshots, transcripts, or chat contents outside the meeting without permission from the people involved.
  • Only share your screen when you mean to. Close private windows and notifications first.

6. No Spam or Disruption

  • No unsolicited advertising, mass invitations, or repetitive messages.
  • No "meeting bombing" — joining meetings you were not invited to in order to disrupt them.
  • No deliberately flooding chat, reactions, or screen sharing to make a meeting unusable.

7. Protect Younger Users

Liqaa is not intended for anyone under 13. If you believe a younger child is using the service, please report it so we can act.

8. Hosting a Meeting Responsibly

If you host, you have tools to keep your meeting safe:

  • Require a password for meetings that should not be public.
  • Turn on the waiting room so you approve each person before they join.
  • Lock the meeting once everyone has arrived.
  • Share meeting links privately rather than posting them publicly.

9. Reporting and Blocking

If someone breaks these guidelines:

  • Use the Report option in the participants panel or on a contact to flag the behaviour.
  • Use Block to stop someone contacting you or inviting you to meetings.
  • Leave the meeting if you feel unsafe — your safety comes before the meeting.

Reports include the reason you select and any details you add. Blocking takes effect immediately on your device.

10. What Happens When Guidelines Are Broken

Depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is, we may:

  • Issue a warning.
  • Restrict access to certain features.
  • Suspend or permanently terminate the account.
  • Refer the matter to law enforcement where the law requires it.

11. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these guidelines as Liqaa grows. Continuing to use the service after an update means you accept the revised guidelines.

12. Questions

If something here is unclear, reach out through the Help & Support page. See also our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
