Liqaa — Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Liqaa uses cookies, browser storage, and similar technologies when you visit our website or use our video conferencing service. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies and Browser Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. "Browser storage" refers to related technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage, which let a website save information directly in your browser.

Liqaa relies mainly on browser storage rather than traditional tracking cookies.

2. What We Store and Why

Strictly Necessary Storage

These are required for Liqaa to function. Without them, you cannot sign in or hold a meeting.

  • Session data — keeps you signed in on this device.
  • Account records — your name, email, and account settings saved locally in this browser.
  • Meeting history — meetings you created or joined, so you can rejoin them quickly.
  • Contacts — the people you added to your contact list.
  • Verification codes — short-lived codes used to verify your email or reset your password. These expire after 10 minutes.
  • Join preferences — whether you join meetings with your mic and camera on.

Preference Storage

  • Theme — whether you prefer the light or dark appearance.
  • Language — whether you use Liqaa in Arabic or English.
  • Device selection — your preferred camera, microphone, and speaker.

Analytics and Advertising

Liqaa does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or third-party marketing trackers.

3. Third-Party Services

Some features rely on third-party providers that may set their own cookies or storage when they are used:

  • LiveKit — powers real-time audio, video, and screen sharing.
  • EmailJS — delivers verification and password reset emails.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typeface used across the interface.

These providers process data under their own privacy and cookie policies.

4. How Long Data Is Kept

  • Session storage is cleared when you close the browser tab.
  • Preferences and account data remain until you delete your account or clear your browser data.
  • Verification and password reset codes expire after 10 minutes.

5. Managing and Deleting Stored Data

You are always in control:

  • Clear your browser data (cookies and site data) to remove everything Liqaa has stored on this device.
  • Use Settings → Danger zone → Delete account to erase your Liqaa account and its data from this device.
  • Use Settings → Danger zone → Clear meeting history to remove your meeting list only.

Blocking all storage will prevent you from signing in and using meetings, because sign-in state cannot be remembered.

6. Do Not Track

Liqaa does not track you across other websites, so there is no cross-site behaviour to disable.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this Cookie Policy, please reach us through the Help & Support page.
